01 · WHO WE ARE

Change begins when we act together.

Founded on September 23, 2020 by Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. The organisation works with volunteers, professionals and partners to address community needs across India.

Its work spans food and clothing support, education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability and employability-focused skill development.
